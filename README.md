 #THE FACILITIES MANAGEMENT AND MAINTENANCE COMPANY PROJECT OVERVIEW
 
 ##PROJECT OVERVIEW
 
This project analyses the operational and business perfomance of a facilities management and maintenance company across
its various projects.

The objective is to transform project data into meaningful insights that help management understand perfomance,monitor key perfomance 
indicators(KPIs)identifying areas that requiring improvement and support data driven decison-making.

The Dashboard provides an overview of perfomance across different projects,enabling stakeholders to compare results,monitor operational
 efficiency and identify opportunities for improvement.
 
##BUSINESS OBJECTIVES

The main objectives of these project is to:
a)Evaluate the perfomance of different facilities management and maintenance projects
b)Monitor key perfomance indicators across projects
c)Compare projects perfomance and identify high and low perfomimg areas
d)Identify operational trends and potential areas of inefficiency.
e)Provides insight that can support management planning and decision making.

##TOOLS USED

EXCEL;for data visualsation,dashboard development and repoting.
Python;for data cleaning and analysis
SQL;for data querying
POWER BI;for data visualisation,dashboad development and reporting.

##KEY INSIGHTS

The analysis aims to identify:
a)Differences in perfomance across diffence projects
b)Projects perfoming well against selected KPIs
c)Areas where operational perfomance may require improvement
d)Trends and patterns that can help the management allocate resources more effectively

##CONCLUSION

This project demonstrates how data analysis and dashboard reporting can be used to help a facilities management
and maintenance company monitor a project perfomance,identify a project operational improvement opportunities and
and support informed business decisions.

The project also demonstrate practical skills in data analysis,perfomance monitoring ,data visualisation and business
intelligence.
